8x friends · interactive prototype
The graph is the app. Every flow happens inside it.
No screens stacked on screens — one living force simulation that re-forms itself for each task. Focus, logging, nudges, group-building and time travel are all the same graph under different forces, so nothing ever cuts. Everything is live: drag a node and the springs answer.
The decay language
Fresh links carry light. Packets run along the edge and the node core breathes. Your eye goes to motion first.
Fading links thin and dim as weeks pass, and the spring lengthens — the person physically drifts away from you.
Dead links deconstruct. The line breaks into drifting fragments and the countdown ring around the node empties into amber. It's the only warm colour in the app, so it's impossible to miss.
Three directions for the home graph
WEB — free force layout. Truest picture of who knows who; clusters emerge on their own.
ORBIT — you at the centre, radius = time since you last met. Drifting people literally fall to the outer rings.
STRATA — islands by shared context (family, climbing, work…). Best for spotting a group you could assemble.
Switching re-runs the same simulation with different target forces, so the graph reassembles instead of cutting. Try it mid-drag.
Try this
1 · Tap a dim node on the edge — the graph zooms and its neighbours arrange around it.
2 · LOG → tap three people → they huddle → confirm and watch the links re-ignite.
3 · PULL — the three most-faded people stretch away on elastic tethers.
4 · TIME → drag the scrubber and watch 18 months of the graph decay and refresh.
